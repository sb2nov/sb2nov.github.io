I'm an engineer, founder, and investor. I love building things that matter, especially when there's an ambitious mission behind them.

Most recently, I co-founded [Uplimit](https://uplimit.com/) and served as CTO through its acquisition by [Handshake](https://joinhandshake.com/). We built an AI tutor that could talk with learners, create interactive lessons, and adapt to how they learn. Companies like Databricks, GE HealthCare, and Procore used it to train their teams. At Handshake, I led engineering for its learning products.

Before that, I spent much of my career building machine learning systems. At Google Brain, I helped bring TPUs to TensorFlow and worked on Apache Beam. At Neeva, I led personalization and data infrastructure. At Coursera, I built data infrastructure and machine learning products, from recommendations to automatically generated quizzes.

These days, I'm especially curious about AI for science, and I've always been fascinated by what makes great companies tick.
